CHAPTER 02 · OWNERSHIP

The things I need to
take responsibility for.

Not to punish myself. Not to make you feel sorry for me. I am saying these things because real change starts when I stop defending my mistakes and start owning them.

01/09
SWIPE · DRAG · USE THE ARROWS
MY RESPONSIBILITY
01LISTENING
👂
I SHOULD HAVE PAUSED

I reacted instead of listening.

There were moments when I answered from emotion before really understanding what you were trying to tell me.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADListen fully. Then answer — not the other way around.
02ARGUMENTS
🌊
I SHOULD HAVE SOFTENED

I let disagreements grow.

Some arguments became bigger than they needed to be because I did not know when to pause and soften my approach.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADLower the temperature before trying to solve the problem.
03WORDS
💬
I SHOULD HAVE PROTECTED YOUR HEART

I let anger choose my words.

There are things I said in heated moments that I regret. Words stay long after an argument ends.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADWalk away from the argument before anger turns into hurt.
04COMMUNICATION
✉️
I SHOULD HAVE SPOKEN CLEARLY

I did not communicate clearly.

I sometimes kept things inside, assumed things, or expected you to understand feelings I had not explained.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADSay what I feel clearly instead of making you guess.
05DEFENSIVENESS
🫶
I SHOULD HAVE HEARD YOU FIRST

I became defensive.

I sometimes explained myself before acknowledging how my actions affected you. You deserved to be heard first.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADAcknowledge your feelings first. Explain myself second.
06PRIDE
🥀
I SHOULD HAVE LET MY EGO GO

I let pride get in the way.

There were moments when saying “I was wrong” should have been easier than protecting my ego.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADChoose the relationship over the need to look right.
07BEING RIGHT
🤝
I SHOULD HAVE CHOSEN US

I tried to win conversations.

A relationship should not have winners and losers. I should have focused on solving things together.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADStop treating you like the opponent. Solve the problem with you.
08PATIENCE
🕊️
I SHOULD HAVE GIVEN YOU TIME

I rushed things.

I sometimes wanted an immediate solution when you may simply have needed time to process.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADRespect your space without turning it into another argument.
09SHOWING LOVE
💗
I SHOULD HAVE MADE IT FELT

I did not always show love the way you needed.

Caring about someone and making them feel cared for are not always the same thing.

WHAT I SHOULD DO INSTEADMake love visible through consistency, not only through words.
And what I want to practice from here ♡
Listen before answering Pause before reacting Speak clearly Respect your space Stop keeping score Show consistency

TO · Kunnu 🥰FROM · Boii 😘😍
A letter I wish I could place gently in your hands

My Kuttu 🥰,

my Kanna · my Kunnu

I don't know whether a website can repair what two people have struggled to repair with words. Maybe it can't. But I know I don't want silence, distance, or difficult days to be the only things left between us.

So I wanted to give you something that feels slower than a text message — something you can read when you are ready, without me standing there waiting for an answer.

I'm sorry. For the moments my words hurt you. For the times I reacted instead of listening. For the times fear, anger, pride, assumptions, or my own stubbornness became bigger than the love I was trying to protect.

I know sorry does not erase what happened. And I don't want forgiveness to become another thing I ask you to give me on my timeline. You are allowed to feel hurt. You are allowed to be angry. You are allowed to take time.

I don't want to win against you.
I want us to stop being on opposite sides.

Because I remember us too. I remember the silly conversations, the little things only we understood, the moments that were ordinary to everyone else but somehow special because they were ours.

I can't promise that I will never make another mistake. That would be an empty promise. What I can promise is that I will keep working on the things that hurt us: listening before reacting, communicating instead of assuming, pausing when anger takes over, respecting your need for space, and choosing understanding over pride.

I don't want to prove that I am right. I want to become someone who makes it easier for you to feel heard, respected, safe, and loved.

♡

And Kuttu… whatever name you call me back by, I hope you know this much: the person who calls you Kuttu, Kanna, and Kunnu does it because those names feel like home in my heart.

I don't know what your answer will be. I won't force one. I only hope that one day, when you think about us, you remember that there was someone willing to look at his mistakes honestly and try to do better — not just say better things.

Always hoping for us,

Your Boii 😘😍♡ for my Kuttu · my Kanna · my Kunnu ♡
